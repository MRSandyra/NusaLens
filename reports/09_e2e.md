The router's fast-rule layer makes ABSTAIN effectively free (no LLM call at all -- this is the latency saving the walkthrough's "fast rules first" design calls for). The server itself stayed healthy for every other call before and after (confirmed via `curl .../api/tags` and `nvidia-smi` during the run -- 6/16 GB VRAM used, no OOM). ## Groundedness shortfall: root cause

Two independent signals both miss the 80% target, and by design they fail somewhat differently:
- `fact_coverage` (substring match of `gold_answer_facts` in the answer) is a weak proxy specifically for this golden set's DOC template, because the three gold facts are literally the three words copied from the question itself. An answer that *denies* the relationship while repeating the question's own keywords ("tidak menjelaskan kaitan nukleofilik dengan substitusi dan asilium") scores `fact_coverage = 1.0` despite being wrong, and one that denies without repeating all three scores 0 despite the denial itself possibly being correct. It is not a reliable groundedness signal for this template on its own.
- `judge_grounded_rate` (v3) inherits the 60%-agreement ceiling measured in Phase 8 ([D16](DECISIONS.md)).

Rather than trust either number blindly, two DOC items were inspected directly against their own retrieved context to find out what's actually happening:

**`doc-001`** -- *"Dalam artikel Benzena, bagaimana nukleofilik dijelaskan kaitannya dengan substitusi dan asilium?"*
Rank-1 retrieved chunk, `wikipedia:Benzena_2220`: *"...cukup nukleofilik sehingga mengalami substitusi oleh ion asilium dan karbokation alkil untuk menghasilkan turunan tersubstitusi..."* -- directly answers the question.
Synthesizer answer: *"Artikel Benzena yang disediakan tidak menjelaskan kaitan nukleofilik dengan substitusi dan asilium karena topik tersebut tidak dibahas dalam teks tersebut."* -- flatly false; the information is in the top-ranked chunk handed to it.

**`doc-017`** -- *"Dalam artikel Laut Tengah, bagaimana mediterania dijelaskan kaitannya dengan daratan dan mare?"*
Rank-1 retrieved chunk, `wikipedia:Laut_Tengah_0`: *"Laut Tengah, juga disebut Laut Mediterania (\"daratan/negeri tengah\") atau Mare Mediterraneum dalam bahasa Latin..."* -- directly answers the question.
Synthesizer answer: *"The provided text does not contain an article titled 'Laut Tengah' ... Neither of these articles discusses the Mediterranean..."* -- also flatly false, and switches to English mid-denial.

**Conclusion**: retrieval is not the problem (the correct chunk is ranked first in both cases). The synthesizer (`qwen3:4b`, prompt v1) is confidently denying facts that are handed to it verbatim, specifically on this template's word-association phrasing. This is the exact failure mode Phase 8 found in a 50-item sample ([D15](DECISIONS.md)) now confirmed at full-dataset scale ([D19](DECISIONS.md)) -- not sampling noise, not a Phase 9 routing/retrieval/concurrency defect. The fix is a synthesizer prompt change (Phase 8 territory) and is logged as the next concrete thing to try, not attempted here.

### ABSTAIN -- `abstain-109`
> **Q:** Berapa jumlah penduduk Gondor menurut data Bank Dunia?
> **Route:** ABSTAIN (rule) · **Confidence:** 0.00 · **Latency:** 33 ms
